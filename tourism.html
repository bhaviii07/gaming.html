Travel Tips

Keep these simple tips in mind before starting your journey.

  • Plan your trip and check the weather before travelling.
  • Carry identification, necessary medicines and drinking water.
  • Respect local traditions, customs and religious places.
  • Keep historical monuments and natural surroundings clean.
  • Check entry fees, opening hours and transport options in advance.
  • Try local food and support local businesses.
